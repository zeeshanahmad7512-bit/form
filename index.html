Our Story

A Family Farm Rooted in Tradition

AMMAR Dairy Farm began with a simple belief — that fresh, honest dairy should be part of every family's day. For over a decade, our family has tended the land and cared for our animals with respect and devotion.

From early morning milking to afternoon grazing on lush pastures, every step of our process honours the natural rhythm of farm life. The result is milk that tastes the way nature intended.
