Our Delivery Service

The AMMAR Milk Round

Our milk round is a traditional doorstep delivery service, reimagined for modern families. We bring fresh milk, cream, yogurt and more straight to your home — before you even wake up.

No supermarket queues, no plastic waste, no compromise on freshness. Just pure, natural dairy from our farm to your family, delivered with a smile.

Delivered Before 7 AM

Fresh milk on your doorstep by the time you start your day.

Flexible Schedule

Choose your delivery days and skip or pause anytime.

Eco-Friendly Bottles

Reusable glass bottles collected and refilled — zero waste.
